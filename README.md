# Excel-Sales-Dashboard-Project
An interactive Excel Sales Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, track profit, identify business trends, and visualize key metrics across product categories, regions, and time periods using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.

## Overview

The **ABC Executive Sales Trend Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel. The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, tracking revenue and profitability, and identifying business trends through data visualization.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful insights using interactive filters, key performance indicators (KPIs), and visual reports to support data-driven business decisions.

## Dataset

The project uses a sales dataset containing information related to:

- Product Categories
- Countries
- Quarters
- Monthly Sales
- Revenue and Gross Profit
- Sales Channels
- Regional Performance

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data analysis and dashboard creation.
- **Pivot Tables** – Data summarization and analysis.
- **Pivot Charts** – Sales data visualization.
- **Excel Slicers** – Interactive filtering.
- **Excel Formulas** – Calculations and KPI analysis.
- **Map Chart** – Geographical sales visualization.

## Project Steps

1. **Data Preparation:** Organized the sales dataset for analysis.
2. **Data Analysis:** Used Pivot Tables to summarize sales information.
3. **KPI Calculation:** Analyzed revenue, gross profit, profit margin, units sold, orders, and COGS.
4. **Data Visualization:** Created charts to represent sales trends and performance.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Designed an interactive executive dashboard.
6. **Project Documentation:** Prepared a project report describing the analysis and dashboard.

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Total Units Sold
- Total Orders
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

### Data Visualizations

- **Monthly Revenue and Profit:** Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- **Top 5 Countries by Revenue:** Compares revenue performance across the top five countries.
- **Revenue Share by Channel:** Displays revenue distribution across sales channels.
- **Region-wise Comparison:** Compares sales performance across European regions.
- **Country-wise Map Chart:** Visualizes geographical sales performance.

### Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides slicers for:
- Product Category
- Country
- Quarter

These filters allow users to explore sales performance across different business dimensions.

## Results

The visualizations provide an overview of monthly sales trends, revenue distribution, regional performance, and country-wise sales.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, businesses can:

- Monitor monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Evaluate the performance of different sales channels.
- Identify countries with strong revenue contributions.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Track profitability using gross profit margin.
- Use interactive filters to support business planning and decision-making.
